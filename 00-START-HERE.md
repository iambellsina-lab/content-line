# Start here

Everything in this folder, in the order to use it. **Twenty minutes to be running.**

---

## Right now, in order

### 1. Take the zip, not the folder

`content-line-kit.zip`. One download, everything inside, correct folder structure. Unzip it somewhere
you will keep it. Downloading 33 files one at a time is the slow way to do the same thing.

### 2. Read `THE-SYSTEM.md`. Five minutes

**The map.** Three tools, one job each, and how they fit:

- **GoHighLevel is the spine.** It holds the client, the money and the publishing. It is the only one of
  the three that knows whether a post produced revenue.
- **Claude is the engine.** It researches the niche, edits the footage, writes the captions, renders the
  files.
- **Metricool is optional.** Free at the tier that matters. It adds per-post numbers GoHighLevel does
  not have. Not required to run the system. Required to improve it.

### 3. Run `setup.sh`

It checks every tool and tells you exactly what is missing. Four things, all free: Claude Code, ffmpeg
with libx264, Python 3, and optionally whisper-cli for transcription. `README.md` section 2 has the
exact commands for Mac, Windows and Linux.

**The Windows install has not been tested by anyone.** Everything here was built and proven on a Mac. If
you are on Windows, expect to hit something, and say what it was.

### 4. Fill in `BRAND-INTAKE.md`. Fifteen minutes, once per client

**This is the front door and the step people skip.** Six questions only the client can answer, and three
things you point at rather than describe.

Skip it and everything the system makes will be competent and generic, which is worse than nothing
because it looks like effort.

## The honest state of it

**What works and is proven:** rendering, captioning, colour grading, local transcription, niche
measurement on YouTube with real view counts, and the editing line end to end.

**What is not settled, and is written up inside rather than hidden:** the research skill currently ranks
posts on views, saves and shares, which are attention metrics. It will reliably find a popular account
that sells nothing. `WHAT-PRODUCES-MONEY.md` explains why that matters and what the evidence says to
measure instead. That rewrite is in progress.

**Everything runs locally and costs nothing per use.** The only recurring costs are tools a business was
already paying for.
